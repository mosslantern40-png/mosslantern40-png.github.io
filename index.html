CareLedger Privacy Policy

Effective date: 6 October 2026

CareLedger is an Android appointment and customer management app published under the developer name CareLedger.

Privacy contact: careledger.app@outlook.com

1. Overview

CareLedger is designed as a local-first app. Customer, appointment and business records are stored on the Android device running CareLedger unless the user deliberately exports or backs them up.

CareLedger does not require a CareLedger cloud account for its core appointment and customer management features.

2. Information CareLedger handles

Depending on how the user uses the app, CareLedger may process:

  • customer names
  • customer telephone numbers
  • customer email addresses
  • appointment dates, times, services, prices, payment status and notes
  • business name and practitioner name
  • service presets and working-hour settings
  • message status/history relating to SMS reminders and email confirmations
  • SMTP server settings and credentials entered by the user
  • locally generated receipts, invoices, exports and encrypted backups
  • Google Play subscription entitlement information needed to determine access to CareLedger Pro

The user decides what customer and appointment information to enter.

3. Where information is stored

Core business data is stored locally on the user's Android device in CareLedger's private app storage.

Android system backup is disabled for CareLedger. The app instead provides its own user-controlled backup and export tools.

If the user creates an encrypted CareLedger backup, export, receipt or invoice, the resulting file is stored only in the location the user selects or has configured for CareLedger storage.

4. SMS reminders

If the user enables SIM SMS reminders and grants Android SMS permission, CareLedger can send appointment reminders using the device's selected SIM and mobile network.

CareLedger does not use an external CareLedger SMS gateway. The destination telephone number and message content are submitted to the device's Android SMS service and mobile network.

SMS sending is optional and disabled until the user configures and enables it.

5. Email confirmations

If the user configures email sending, CareLedger connects directly to the SMTP server chosen by the user.

Recipient email addresses, message content and appointment calendar information are sent to that SMTP/email provider so the requested email can be delivered.

CareLedger does not operate its own email relay service.

SMTP credentials entered by the user are stored in protected app storage on the Android device. Email sending is optional and disabled until configured and enabled by the user.

6. Google Play Billing

CareLedger Pro subscriptions are handled through Google Play.

When subscription features are used, Google Play may process purchase, account, payment, device and entitlement information under Google's own privacy terms.

CareLedger uses Google Play Billing information only to determine subscription entitlement and does not use the purchase to back up or sync customer or appointment records.

7. Permissions

  • Internet access — for user-configured SMTP email and Google Play Billing
  • SMS permission — only for optional SIM-based appointment reminders
  • Notification permission — for app notifications where supported by Android

Permissions can be denied or changed in Android settings. Some related CareLedger features will then be unavailable.

8. Sharing of information

CareLedger does not sell customer or user data.

CareLedger does not transmit the local customer/appointment database to the CareLedger developer as part of normal app operation.

Information is shared only when the user activates a feature that requires it, for example:

  • with the mobile network when sending an SMS
  • with the user's chosen SMTP/email provider when sending email
  • with Google Play when using subscription and billing services
  • with a destination chosen by the user when exporting, sharing, backing up or restoring files

The privacy practices of those third-party services are governed by their own terms and policies.

9. Security

CareLedger uses Android app-private storage for its local database and settings. The app does not permit cleartext network traffic.

CareLedger encrypted backups are protected using the backup password chosen by the user. Users should keep backup passwords separate from backup files.

No security system can guarantee absolute protection. Users remain responsible for protecting their Android device, email account, backup password and exported files.

10. Data retention and deletion

Customer, appointment and settings data remains on the device until the user edits, deletes, clears or uninstalls CareLedger, subject to Android storage behaviour.

Users can permanently delete supported records within the app. Users can also remove CareLedger's local app data using Android system settings or by uninstalling the app.

Files exported or backed up outside CareLedger remain in the location selected by the user until the user deletes them.

CareLedger does not provide a CareLedger cloud account, so there is no separate CareLedger online account to delete.

Google Play subscription and transaction records are retained by Google according to Google's policies and legal obligations.

11. Children's privacy

CareLedger is intended for business/professional use and is not directed at children.

12. International use

CareLedger can be used in different countries. Any information sent through SMS, email, Google Play or user-selected storage services may be processed by those service providers in accordance with their own locations and privacy policies.

13. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when CareLedger features, legal requirements or third-party services change. The effective date at the top of this page will be updated when material changes are made.

14. Contact

For privacy questions about CareLedger, contact careledger.app@outlook.com.
